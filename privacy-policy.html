We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe when you use our dictionary app.

1 Information We Collect

When you register and use the app, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: email address and display name (when signing up via email or Google Sign-In).
  • Usage data: words you look up, saved word lists, and learning progress.
  • Device information: device type, iOS version, and app instance identifier (non-personally identifiable).
  • Crash reports (optional): technical information when the app encounters an error, used solely for debugging purposes.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any other sensitive personal information.

2 How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes only:

  • To authenticate and manage your user account.
  • To sync your learning data (saved words, streaks, progress) across your devices.
  • To improve app features and overall user experience.
  • To send study reminder notifications (only if you explicitly grant permission).
  • To diagnose technical issues and provide customer support.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to any third party.

3 Firebase and Third-Party Services

The app uses Firebase (Google LLC) for the following functionality:

  • Firebase Authentication: manages sign-in via email/password or Google Sign-In.
  • Cloud Firestore: stores user data including word lists and app settings.

Firebase is operated by Google LLC and governed by Google's privacy policies. Data may be stored on servers in the United States or other regions based on Firebase configuration.

You can review Firebase's privacy policy at: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

The app does not integrate any advertising networks or other third-party analytics SDKs beyond Firebase.

4 Data Storage and Security

We apply appropriate technical measures to protect your data:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Firebase Security Rules enforce per-user data access controls, ensuring users cannot access each other's data.
  • Passwords are hashed and encrypted by Firebase Authentication — we never store passwords in plain text.

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. When you delete your account, all associated data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

5 Sharing of Information

We do not share, sell, or disclose your personal information to third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your explicit prior consent.
  • When required to comply with a legal obligation or a lawful request from a competent authority.
  • With technical service providers (such as Firebase/Google) used solely to operate the app, under strict data processing agreements.

6 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data at any time:

  • Access: view your stored data directly within the app.
  • Correction: update your account information via the Settings screen.
  • Deletion: delete your account and all associated data through the app's Settings, or by contacting us directly.
  • Data portability: contact us to request an export of your personal data.

7 Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you via an in-app notice or email. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

Your continued use of the app after the revised policy takes effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
